# Contributing

Product Sense Practice welcomes feedback from candidates, interviewers, coaches, and nontechnical users. You do not need to write code.

## Report a reproducible problem

Please include:

- the Product Sense question;
- the mode: Candidate or Instructor;
- the affected stage;
- what you expected;
- what actually happened;
- a short, sanitized excerpt that reproduces the issue;
- the ChatGPT model and date, if known.

Remove employer-confidential information, interview questions you are not permitted to share, personal details, customer data, and private ChatGPT links before posting publicly.

## What makes a useful change

Favor fixes for repeated reasoning or usability failures. Preserve:

- the `New` / `Candidate` / `Instructor` workflow;
- stage-by-stage interaction and checkpoints;
- the separation between spoken Candidate delivery and coaching marked **not spoken**;
- meaningful user, problem, and solution prioritization;
- credible breadth without mandatory idea counts or moonshots;
- flexibility when multiple answers are defensible.

A different segment or feature is not automatically a framework bug. Show why the behavior weakens customer insight, prioritization, coherence, learning, pacing, or interview usability across more than one question.

## Proposing a documentation change

Open an issue with the exact page and confusing passage, or submit a focused pull request. Keep beginner setup instructions free of terminal-only requirements.

## Third-party material

Do not submit copied article text, paid newsletter content, spreadsheet copies, private transcripts, employer interview banks, logos, or testimonials without documented permission. Link to primary sources instead.
