# Examples

These focused examples illustrate how the interaction is meant to feel. They do not prescribe the only valid segment, problem, solution, or wording.

- [`candidate-example.md`](candidate-example.md) shows one Candidate stage with a separate decision, learning scaffold, pacing estimate, and checkpoint.
- [`instructor-example.md`](instructor-example.md) shows a user's weak segmentation and a targeted Instructor response that preserves candidate ownership.

Both examples were newly written as public-safe illustrations on 13 September 2026. They are not historical evaluation outputs, and they do not claim live ChatGPT verification.

Do not upload the examples for normal use. The recommended instruction file plus `PROJECT_SETUP.txt` is sufficient.
