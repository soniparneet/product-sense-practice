# Credits and attribution

Product Sense Practice is an independent learning project. It operationalizes and adapts a credited Product Sense framework into a conversational practice workflow; it does not claim to have invented Product Sense interviewing or the underlying framework.

## Framework foundation

- **Ben Erez** authored [The definitive guide to mastering product sense interviews](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/the-definitive-guide-to-mastering) and the linked [Product Sense Interview Template](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1-jdZAVtLuk8dUnf9peUKlNkguSOuAk5VdMPTmgr9Kns/edit?gid=0#gid=0), which provide the foundation for the reasoning stages and interview structure used here.
- **Lenny's Newsletter and Lenny Rachitsky** published the guide.
- The guide also links a [completed VR-for-elderly example](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1WV2kQNdM_kKHvr6Wsq0Znx7-r1gLK0qTOcuZg3LPqwo/edit?gid=0#gid=0).

Please use those primary links for the source material. This repository does not redistribute the article's worked answers, copies of the spreadsheets, article text, or screenshots.

## Project adaptation

- **[soniparneet](https://github.com/soniparneet)**: product design, framework adaptation, learning experience, iterative evaluation, and public documentation.
- **ChatGPT and Codex**: AI assistance with drafting, critique, instruction revision, testing support, and repository implementation.

They should not be read as verbatim rules from, or endorsements by, Ben Erez, Lenny's Newsletter, Stripe, Meta, Netflix, Anthropic, OpenAI, or any other company named in an example.
